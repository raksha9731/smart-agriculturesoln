About Our Project 🌾

Smart Agriculture Solution – Technology for Better Farming

📌 Problem Statement

The community faces significant challenges due to the misuse and overuse of chemical pesticides, leading to reduced soil fertility, crop damage, and health risks for farmers and consumers. There is limited access to reliable and crop-specific guidance on safe and effective pesticide usage.

This project aims to develop a user-friendly advisory system that provides accurate recommendations on pesticide selection and dosage based on crop type, pest identification, and local conditions, promoting sustainable and safe farming practices.

🎯 Project Objectives

01

Reduce Overuse of Pesticides

Provide accurate, crop-specific pesticide recommendations to minimize excessive usage.

02

Improve Crop Health and Yield

Help farmers select the right pesticide and dosage, reducing crop damage and increasing productivity.

03

Promote Safe and Sustainable Farming

Encourage environmentally friendly practices and reduce health risks for farmers, consumers, and the ecosystem.

💡 Proposed Solution

Our Smart Agriculture Solution is a web application designed to assist farmers with agricultural information. It includes weather updates, irrigation guidance, crop recommendations, soil information, and basic pest symptom guidance through a simple dashboard.
